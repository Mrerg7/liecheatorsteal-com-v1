High-profile prosecutions of Donald Trump were brought by Democratic prosecutors in Democratic jurisdictions using novel legal theories, timed to interfere with elections. Meanwhile, Hunter Biden's documented foreign influence peddling and gun violations received delayed and lenient treatment until political pressure mounted. The justice system became a campaign tool.
