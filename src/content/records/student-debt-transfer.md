Multiple executive attempts to cancel or transfer hundreds of billions in student loans onto taxpayers who never took those loans or already paid theirs. This was a direct wealth transfer from working-class families to college-educated borrowers — many with higher future earnings. The Supreme Court blocked the largest version, but the intent to steal from one group to buy votes from another was clear.
