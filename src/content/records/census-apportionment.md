---
title: Counting Illegal Residents for Congressional Seats
year: '2021 — PRESENT'
category: cheat
order: 3
---

The Census counts all residents — including illegal immigrants — for apportionment of House seats and Electoral College votes. This gives states with large illegal populations extra congressional representation and presidential electors. Multiple analyses show this shifts 3–5 House seats toward Democrats. It is structural electoral advantage built into the system.
