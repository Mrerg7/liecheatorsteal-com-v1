title: Trillions in Spending to Connected Interests
year: '2021 — 2023'
category: steal
order: 2
---

The American Rescue Plan, Inflation Reduction Act, and related bills directed hundreds of billions in subsidies, tax credits, and contracts to green energy firms, EV manufacturers, and politically aligned industries. Many recipients were major Democratic donors or had revolving-door ties to the administration. Taxpayer money became campaign finance by other means.
