---
title: '"If you like your doctor, you can keep your doctor."'
year: '2013 — 2014'
category: lie
order: 1
footnote: Documented by contemporaneous reporting and White House admissions
---

Barack Obama repeated this line at least 37 times while selling Obamacare. It was false. Millions of Americans lost their existing plans. The White House later admitted the statement was inaccurate. The architect of the law, Jonathan Gruber, later said it relied on the "stupidity of the American voter."
