---
title: 'The Hunter Biden Laptop Was "Russian Disinformation"'
year: 'OCT 2020'
category: lie
order: 2
---

51 former intelligence officials signed a public letter suggesting the New York Post story about Hunter Biden's laptop had "all the classic earmarks of Russian disinformation." The laptop was later authenticated by multiple media outlets and federal investigators. The letter was used to suppress the story weeks before the election.
