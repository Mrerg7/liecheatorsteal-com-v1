---
title: Changing Election Rules by Fiat
year: '2020 — 2021'
category: cheat
order: 1
---

Multiple states altered voting rules for the 2020 election through executive action, court orders, or agency decisions rather than state legislatures as required by the U.S. Constitution. Critics documented last-minute expansions of mail-in voting and relaxed verification that disproportionately benefited one side. The process itself became the cheat.
